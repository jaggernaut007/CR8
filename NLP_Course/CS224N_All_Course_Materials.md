## Suggested Readings by Lecture

### Lecture 1 — Word Vectors I

1. [Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space (Word2Vec)](http://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.3781.pdf)
2. [Distributed Representations of Words and Phrases and their Compositionality (Negative Sampling)](http://papers.nips.cc/paper/5021-distributed-representations-of-words-and-phrases-and-their-compositionality.pdf)

### Lecture 2 — Word Vectors II & Language Models

1. [GloVe: Global Vectors for Word Representation](http://nlp.stanford.edu/pubs/glove.pdf)
2. [Improving Distributional Similarity with Lessons Learned from Word Embeddings](http://www.aclweb.org/anthology/Q15-1016)
3. [Evaluation Methods for Unsupervised Word Embeddings](http://www.aclweb.org/anthology/D15-1036)

6. [On the Dimensionality of Word Embedding](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/7368-on-the-dimensionality-of-word-embedding.pdf)

### Lecture 3 — Backpropagation & Neural Networks

1. [CS231n Notes on Network Architectures](http://cs231n.github.io/neural-networks-1/)
2. [CS231n Notes on Backprop](http://cs231n.github.io/optimization-2/)

6. [Natural Language Processing (Almost) from Scratch](http://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume12/collobert11a/collobert11a.pdf)

### Lecture 4 — Dependency Parsing

1. [Incrementality in Deterministic Dependency Parsing](https://www.aclweb.org/anthology/W/W04/W04-0308.pdf)

3. [Globally Normalized Transition-Based Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.06042.pdf)
4. [Universal Stanford Dependencies](http://nlp.stanford.edu/~manning/papers/USD_LREC14_UD_revision.pdf)
5. [Universal Dependencies Website](http://universaldependencies.org/)

3. [Deep Learning Book — Sequence Modeling: Recurrent and Recursive Nets (Sections 10.1–10.2)](http://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/rnn.html)
4. [On Chomsky and the Two Cultures of Statistical Learning (Norvig)](http://norvig.com/chomsky.html)

### Lecture 6 — Advanced RNNs & Attention

1. [Learning Long-term Dependencies with Gradient Descent is Difficult](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/279181)
